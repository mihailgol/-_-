## Overview & Curriculum Architecture

This document presents the complete FIPI-aligned educational curriculum specification for Chemistry (ЕГЭ/ОГЭ). It covers 4 fundamental domains required by the FIPI codifier:

1. **Topic 1 (`chem_structure_periodic`)**: Строение атома и Периодический закон (Atomic Structure and the Periodic Law)
2. **Topic 2 (`chem_bonding_lattices`)**: Химическая связь и кристаллическая решетка (Chemical Bonding and Crystal Lattices)
3. **Topic 3 (`chem_inorganic_classes`)**: Неорганическая химия: Оксиды, Кислоты, Основания, Соли (Inorganic Chemistry: Oxides, Acids, Bases, Salts)
4. **Topic 4 (`chem_organic_basics`)**: Органическая химия: Углеводороды и Кислородсодержащие вещества (Organic Chemistry: Hydrocarbons and Oxygen-Containing Compounds)

Each topic is fully structured with:
- **Theory HTML**: Rich semantic HTML using `<h3>`, `<h4>`, `<div class="note-info-box">`, `<table class="data-table">`, `<ul>`, `<ol>`, `<strong>`, chemical notation, and formulas.
- **Video Metadata**: Complete video metadata (`title`, `instructor`, `duration`, `youtubeId`, `views`, `thumbnail`).
- **Test Questions**: 5 FIPI-level questions per topic with option arrays, zero-indexed `correctIndex`, and detailed step-by-step explanations.
