## Overview & Curriculum Architecture

This document presents the complete FIPI-aligned educational curriculum specification for History (ЕГЭ/ОГЭ). It covers 4 fundamental historical eras required by the FIPI codifier:

1. **Topic 1 (`hist_ancient_rus`)**: Древняя и Удельная Русь IX–XV вв. (Ancient and Appanage Rus IX–XV centuries)
2. **Topic 2 (`hist_tzardom_troubles`)**: Российское государство и Смута XVI–XVII вв. (Russian State and Time of Troubles XVI–XVII centuries)
3. **Topic 3 (`hist_russian_empire`)**: Российская империя XVIII–XIX вв. (Russian Empire XVIII–XIX centuries)
4. **Topic 4 (`hist_russia_xx_century`)**: Россия в XX веке: Революции, СССР и ВОВ (Russia in the 20th Century: Revolutions, USSR, and WWII)

Each topic is fully structured with:
- **Theory HTML**: Rich semantic HTML using `<h3>`, `<h4>`, `<div class="note-info-box">`, `<table class="data-table">`, `<ul>`, `<ol>`, `<strong>`, historical terms, dates, and concepts.
- **Video Metadata**: Complete video metadata (`title`, `instructor`, `duration`, `youtubeId`, `views`, `thumbnail`).
- **Test Questions**: 5 FIPI-level questions per topic (20 total) with option arrays, zero-indexed `correctIndex`, and detailed step-by-step historical explanations.
